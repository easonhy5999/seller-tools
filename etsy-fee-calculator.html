How Etsy fees work

Listing fee: $0.20 per item listed. It renews when the item sells, so every sale pays it again.

Transaction fee: 6.5% of the total the customer pays for the item, including the shipping and gift wrap you charge.

Payment processing: about 3% + $0.25 in the US (varies by country), charged on the full order total.

Offsite Ads: if a sale comes from an Etsy ad on another site, Etsy takes 15% (or 12% for shops over $10,000 a year), capped at $100 per order.

Some countries also add a regulatory operating fee or VAT on fees, so treat the result as a close estimate and check your Etsy payment account for exact numbers.

Why count your time?

If a bracelet takes 25 minutes and you'd value your time at $15 an hour, that's $6.25 of cost. A sale that looks like a $2 profit can be a loss once your time is counted.
