How much should I charge for a 3D print?

Most people price a print by filament alone. A 200 g print uses about $4 of PLA, but filament is only part of the cost. Ten hours of machine time wears out nozzles, belts and the printer itself, and uses power. Your time for setup and cleanup counts too, and some prints fail.

Add those together for your real cost, then add a markup for profit. With the default numbers above, a 10-hour, 200 g print really costs about $13, so you'd quote around $21, not $10.

What markup should I use?

Many sellers use 50–100% on top of real cost for one-off custom prints, and less for large repeat orders. If customers never push back on price, your markup is probably too low.
