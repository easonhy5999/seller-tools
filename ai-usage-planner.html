8 habits that stretch your plan

  1. New topic, new chat. Every message makes Claude re-read the whole chat, so long chats use more each time.
  2. Smallest model that does the job. Quick jobs don't need the biggest model.
  3. High effort only for hard problems. More thinking uses more of your limit.
  4. Edit, don't argue. Wrong answer? Edit your first message instead of adding "no, I meant…".
  5. Batch your questions. Five questions in one message, not five messages.
  6. Put reusable files in a Project. Anthropic says Project content is cached and doesn't count again when reused.
  7. Don't re-upload the same file. It's already in the chat.
  8. Check Settings → Usage. See how much of your session and week you've used before a big job.

Which Claude model should I use?

Claude comes in sizes. The small, fast model (Haiku) is great for quick answers and simple rewrites. The middle one (Sonnet) handles most everyday writing and coding. The biggest (Opus) is for hard reasoning, big codebases and long analysis. Using the biggest model for everything is the fastest way to hit your limit.

Is it Pro or Max?

Pro is $20/month. Max starts at $100/month for 5× Pro's usage. If you only hit the limit now and then, better habits usually fix it. If you hit it every day doing heavy work like coding, Max may be worth it. Check claude.com/pricing for current plans.

How are the numbers worked out?

Rough estimates: about 1.3 tokens per word and about 500 words per page. Real usage also depends on the model, tools, files and thinking effort. Anthropic doesn't publish exact message limits.
